# ⚡ VIBE

> Your ride. Your vibe. No awkward energy.

VIBE is a Gen-Z focused, open-source ride-hailing platform built to make getting from A → B feel less boring.

Clean UI. Fast flows. Zero unnecessary nonsense.

And yes — **VIBE is vibe coded.** 🤖

---

## 🧃 What is VIBE?

Traditional ride-hailing apps can feel like they were designed by a committee.

VIBE is taking a different approach.

We're building a mobility platform that feels:

- ⚡ Fast
- 🎨 Clean
- 🧠 Simple
- 🫶 Human
- 💸 Driver-friendly

The goal isn't to clone existing ride apps.

The goal is to rethink the experience.
